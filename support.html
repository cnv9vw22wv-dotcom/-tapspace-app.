Tapspace help

Support

Need help with Tapspace, an NFC tag, a space, or a workflow? Start with the guidance below. For anything else, contact support directly.

Scanning a tag

Unlock your iPhone, open Tapspace, select the NFC scanner, and hold the top of your phone close to the tag.

Setting up a tag

Create or open an experience, choose “Set up tag,” and follow the on-screen instructions.

NFC is unavailable

Confirm that your iPhone supports NFC, the app has the required permission, and you are using a compatible build from TestFlight or the App Store.

Privacy or deletion

Review the Privacy Policy or contact support for help with a privacy request or deletion issue.

Contact support

Email: support@tapspace.app

Typical response time: within 2 business days.
