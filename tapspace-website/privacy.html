Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Tapspace handles information when you use the Tapspace mobile application and website. Tapspace is designed to help users create NFC-powered spaces, tasks, routines, focus sessions, and personal workflows.

1. Information you provide

Tapspace may store information you choose to create inside the app, such as space names, tasks, routines, timers, notes, preferences, and NFC tag assignments. This content is used to provide the app's core features.

2. Device permissions

Tapspace may request access to NFC capabilities so that you can scan or configure compatible tags. The app only requests permissions needed for features you choose to use. Permission availability may depend on your device and operating system.

3. Information collected automatically

Tapspace does not use personal information for cross-app tracking or targeted advertising. If technical diagnostics or crash information are collected through Apple or a service provider, they are used only to maintain security, reliability, and app performance.

4. How information is used

  • To operate and provide Tapspace features.
  • To save your preferences and app content.
  • To diagnose technical problems and improve reliability.
  • To respond to support requests.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

5. Data sharing

Tapspace does not sell personal data. Information may be shared only with service providers needed to operate the app, when required by law, or when necessary to protect users, Tapspace, or the public.

6. Data retention and deletion

Information is retained only for as long as needed to provide the app, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and maintain security. You may delete content directly inside the app where deletion controls are available. You may also contact support to request assistance.

7. Children's privacy

Tapspace is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. A parent or guardian who believes a child has provided personal information may contact us.

8. International users

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing of personal data. You may contact us to exercise an applicable right.

9. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as Tapspace develops. The revised date will appear at the top of this page.

11. Contact

Privacy questions may be sent to support@tapspace.app. Replace this address with an email account you actively monitor before publishing.
